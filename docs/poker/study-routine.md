# Build Your Routine

Everything in this wiki, assembled into a study system you can run. The key idea: **poker skill mostly decays as *feel* (your fast per-spot intuition), not as forgotten facts** — so maintenance is **re-drilling**, not flashcards. Adapt the volumes to your situation; the *structure* is what matters.

## The skill lifecycle

Every spot you study moves through four stages. This replaces any fixed "study a topic then forget it" calendar:

| Stage | What you do | How |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Acquire** | Build a brand-new spot's feel from scratch | Study the solution first, then **blocked** drilling (one spot, repeated) — [worked examples beat struggling when it's new](../cognitive-load.md) |
| **2. Consolidate** | *Finish* the build — deepen it, make it automatic | **Interleaved** drilling (mix it with other spots) → builds discrimination `[`[interleaving](../interleaving.md)`]` |
| **3. Maintain** | Keep the feel from fading | **Spaced, interleaved re-drills** — revisit at expanding intervals |
| **4. Repair** | Rebuild a spot whose feel has tanked | Drop back to **blocked** drilling on just that spot |

> **The rule: block to acquire & repair, interleave to retain.** A new skill needs concentrated, close-together reps; a *learned* skill is kept sharp by spaced, mixed reps.

## The core loop (one cycle per leak)

```
1. DIAGNOSE  → find your real leaks (tracker vs winners + solver report)
2. ACQUIRE   → study the solution, then drill it closed-book until the feel is there
3. CONSOLIDATE → interleave it with other spots to make it automatic
4. APPLY     → use it in play; mark hands; review them
5. MAINTAIN  → re-drill it at spaced intervals (interleaved); repair any spot that decays
```

Every step maps to a principle: [diagnose](database-review.md) = deliberate practice, [drill closed-book](drilling.md) = retrieval, spaced+interleaved maintenance = [the desirable difficulties](../learning-vs-performance.md), [apply/review](../feedback-and-metacognition.md) = feedback.

## What to use flashcards (Anki) for

This is the part most players get wrong. **Don't flashcard postflop strategy** — a heuristic card is either something you'd never forget or a worse version of a trainer drill, and it can't rebuild *feel* anyway. Use [Anki](anki-for-poker.md) for the two things that genuinely are declarative and forgettable:

1. **Preflop ranges** — exact frequencies and thresholds.
2. *(optional)* **Drill-scheduling** — use spaced-repetition as the *scheduler* that tells you which spot to re-drill, graded by how the drill went.

## Match the task to your energy

- **Fresh:** the *hard* work — acquiring new spots, generative drilling.
- **Tired:** the *light* work — marking hands, journaling, Anki ranges. **Never learn new material fatigued.** → [Attention & Flow](../attention-and-flow.md)

## Distribute, don't cram

- **Short, frequent, focused** beats long marathons — and it builds in [spacing](../spaced-practice.md) automatically.
- **Re-drill at expanding intervals** (days → weeks → month) — let a spaced-repetition scheduler decide what's due, and drill the due set **interleaved**.
- One topic at a time, finished — [protect your attention](../attention-and-flow.md); cut table count when a spot isn't autopilot.

## Study vs volume

- **Volume is necessary** (earnings, variance, reps) — and it *maintains* the spots you play often, for free.
- **Targeted study + feedback is what improves you**, especially once advanced ([more hours barely helps elites](../deliberate-practice.md)).
- The spaced re-drills matter most for your **lower-frequency spots** — the ones volume never reaches.

## Don't forget the two non-negotiables

- **Mental game** — schedule it: tilt review, stop-losses, process over results. → [Mental Game](mental-game.md)
- **Sleep** — it's when learning consolidates. → [Transfer & Sleep](../transfer-and-sleep.md)

## A simple weekly shape (example)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Daily** | A focused drill block when fresh — your current acquisition *or* the spots due for a spaced re-drill (interleaved) + ~10 min Anki ranges |
| **Most days** | Play volume + a quick "mark hands" pass after |
| **Once a week** | A deeper hand-review session (your marked hands, reasoned out then solver-checked) |
| **Monthly** | Re-diagnose leaks; pick the next acquisition target; review tilt & results *as process feedback* |

## Key takeaway

**Diagnose → acquire (blocked) → consolidate (interleaved) → maintain with spaced, interleaved re-drills → repair what decays.** Flashcards are for preflop ranges, not postflop feel. Hard work fresh, light work tired, mental game and sleep protected — and let *leak-shrinkage* and your *drill scores*, not your graph, tell you it's working.
